# FTC fault-DR A/B result 2026-07-27: fault-DR adopted (5-12x less m4-dead degradation, zero terminations, tail removal); privileged fault obs NOT adopted (H2); fault_severity curriculum ended under-expanded at 8-10% of range

[FINDING] Fault-DR ADOPTED. Randomised thruster-health faults under a DORAEMON fault_severity curriculum cut m4-dead attitude degradation 5-12x at none/medium/hard and removed every fault-induced termination, at no cost to healthy attitude accuracy (both arms BEAT the anchor on healthy att_norm ss_error by more than the 0.10 deg floor). soft is the exception: the anchor was already benign there (delta 0.282 deg) so the arms improve only 1.2-2.5x -- do NOT average soft into the headline. [EVIDENCE: att_norm ss_error fault-delta anchor 1.805/0.282/1.818/3.472 deg vs ArmA 0.285/0.241/0.251/0.432 and ArmB 0.148/0.113/0.149/0.669; survival anchor -6.25/0/-4.69/-7.81 pp vs both arms 0.00 pp; min ratio recomputed = 5.19x (att_norm hard ArmB). analysis diagnose-20260727-140324 section generalization] [CONFIDENCE: HIGH]

[FINDING] The mechanism is HEAVY-TAIL REMOVAL, not a mean shift -- reporting only the mean delta understates fault-DR badly. [EVIDENCE: CV = ss_error_std/ss_error healthy->m4-dead at none: anchor roll 0.73->3.34 and pitch 0.22->4.28 (explodes) vs ArmA 0.45->0.48, ArmB 0.29->0.30 (flat); anchor repeats it at medium (roll 0.77->3.96)] [CONFIDENCE: HIGH]

[FINDING] Privileged fault obs (thruster health in the 28D->34D privileged obs) NOT adopted -- H2. On attitude the arm ordering REVERSES across DR levels (A-B on att_norm delta +0.137/+0.129/+0.102/-0.236 deg), failing the sign-consistency bar; on yaw the ordering is stable in Arm A's favour at 4/4 levels but the A-B gap itself is BELOW the pre-registered floor everywhere (os_env_mean gap 1.9-3.1 pp vs 10 pp floor; n_gt20 gap 4-6 envs vs 15-env floor). So H2 rests on the ABSENCE of a floor-clearing advantage for Arm B on either axis, NOT on a decisive Arm A win. Arm A is also the simpler design (28D, nothing for the student to infer). [EVIDENCE: analysis diagnose-20260727-140324; all deltas independently reproduced by constrained_albc/analysis/compare.py paired (commit 890b0e4)] [CONFIDENCE: MEDIUM -- n=1 seed per arm]

[FINDING] The privileged channel DOES take hold mechanically, it just does not convert to robustness: Arm B has 2.0x the encoder gradient norm (0.0751 vs 0.0377/0.0371), better critic convergence (value 0.507 vs 0.549, cost_value 0.611 vs 0.667) and an unstretched thruster_util budget (0.798 vs Arm A 0.902, anchor 0.805 -- Arm A's margin is HALVED because a fault-blind policy must over-drive the survivors). Literature runs the OTHER way: Xu et al. predict continuous-latent adaptation fails on discrete actuator dropout WITHOUT explicit fault conditioning, i.e. Arm B should win -- so this is a TENSION with that prediction at the teacher level, not support for H2. The case Xu actually addresses (the deployable STUDENT inferring fault through a continuous latent) was out of scope and remains untested. [EVIDENCE: TB finals via /isaac-sim/python.sh EventAccumulator; engine TIER 2 Constraints] [CONFIDENCE: HIGH for numbers, MEDIUM for mechanism]

[FINDING] MOST CONSEQUENTIAL: the fault_severity curriculum did NOT stall (mode=0.00, success 0.579/0.753 above alpha 0.5 -- the nominal-0 DORAEMON-knob design worked) but it also never SATURATED -- it ended at 0.0771 (Arm A) / 0.0959 (Arm B) of its [0,1] range, still rising monotonically and convexly at iter 4750. Effective exposure was therefore tiny: per-thruster fail prob u*0.10 = 0.0077/0.0096, so only 4.5-5.6% of envs carried ANY degraded thruster and 2+ simultaneous faults occurred in ~0.09% of envs. The robustness above was bought at under 6% exposure and the ceiling of fault-DR on this plant is UNMEASURED. Next probe = same config run longer (or a faster severity schedule) to find where the curve turns over -- NOT another placement A/B. [EVIDENCE: curriculum_trajectory.json Beta(a,b) vs param_bounds, cross-checked exactly against TB DORAEMON/mean/fault_severity; sampler re-run at run cfg gives per-channel fault rates uniform within 0.15 pp across all 6 channels] [CONFIDENCE: HIGH]

[FINDING] The randomisation is uniform over ALL SIX thrusters -- no channel is privileged. sample_thruster_health draws i.i.d. Bernoulli over shape (num_envs, 6) with severity applied as a per-env scalar broadcast equally across channels. Verified empirically by re-running the real function at the runs' own cfg: per-channel fault rates agree within 0.15 pp and residual-health means are 0.25 on every channel. The eval, however, is m4-ONLY on purpose: sim models the vertical pair (m0/m3) as two independent channels while the real robot has one motor on dual ESC, so a vertical kill would manufacture a pitch-authority loss that does not exist. [EVIDENCE: faults.py:65-70; albc_env.py:1596-1599; 400k-env sampler re-run 2026-07-27; see tam_vertical_single_motor_dual_esc_measured_2026_07_05] [CONFIDENCE: HIGH]
