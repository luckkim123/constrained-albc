# Cross-family adversarial review — p5 teacher, launched 2026-09-07 13:06

## Bottom line

**Overall: NOT TRUSTED AS WRITTEN; no BLOCK.** I found no defect that proves the already-running teacher is worthless as-is, so I do not call for a teacher relaunch. I do find multiple MAJOR defects in the calibration argument, exam validity, health readout, and downstream chain. In particular, the supplied exams cannot be trusted for checkpoint selection, and the automatic chain can stop or bind the student to the wrong checkpoint. The plan itself says this review may be folded in before the student stage (`plan_update.md:173-178`).

## 1. DR criterion across 21+2 dimensions and override ranges

**Verdict: REFUTED.**

The explicit held-outside case is `thrust_coefficient_scale`: the held band is 6.5-26 N, but `finding/146` measures 6.4 N/unit. The plan calls 6.4/13/26.8 N a band that 6.5-26 N “spans”; both endpoints are arithmetically outside, although only 6.4 is a measured real value and 26.8 is identified elsewhere as an unmeasured candidate (`plan_update.md:84-88`; `evidence/SWEEP_native_mac.md:14,29,103-106`; `finding/146`; `finding/160`). This is Finding 10.

The plan also does not show a 23-row application of the criterion. Its dispositions cover inertia, delay, K, thrust, fault probability, velocity caps, and an unmeasured Fz ceiling, then assert coverage of the full set (`plan_update.md:33-104`; `decision_p5.md:5-13`). I found no second unambiguous direct-real-measurement violation after honoring supersessions: K's required 1.56 cm shift is inside the +/-4 cm BG-offset band (`evidence/SWEEP_native_mac.md:20`; `plan_update.md:78-82`), while the apparent 41.7 kg added-mass fit was part of the absolute-value chain later fully retracted by `finding/146` (`evidence/SWEEP_native_mac.md:52-53`; `evidence/SWEEP_native_ml_finding.md:263-264`). The Stonefish damping and geometric buoy-added-mass gaps are not direct measurements of this real robot under the criterion's literal wording (`evidence/SWEEP_native_ml_finding.md:98-100,253-258`).

## 2. Inertia arithmetic and plant consistency

**Verdict: REFUTED overall — scalar arithmetic CONFIRMED; plant-wide nominal consistency CANNOT-DECIDE and is not established.**

At `s=2, a=1.5`, rigid inertia is `0.0994*2 = 0.1988`; the added-mass clamp is `0.95*0.1988 = 0.18886`; requested added mass is `0.09*1.5 = 0.135`. Because 0.135 is below 0.18886, the clamp does not bind and `J=0.1988+0.135=0.3338`, agreeing with the corrected plan (`plan_update.md:55-64`; `evidence/SWEEP_native_ml_finding.md:11,14`; `finding/015`; `finding/312`). At nominal `s=4, a=1`, the same reduced equation gives `0.3976+0.09=0.4876` (`plan_update.md:55-64`).

That calculation is not a demonstrated whole-plant mapping. The 0.49 target is derived from the assembly's measured mode, while the inventory separately reports base rigid 0.0994, assembly-summed dry 0.214, and simulated total 0.320 kg·m² (`evidence/SWEEP_native_mac.md:11-12,109-116`; `finding/145`; `finding/149`). The launched change scales PhysX inertia for both main and buoy bodies, yet the nominal equation counts only one 0.0994 rigid diagonal and one 0.09 added term (`plan_update.md:55-64,112`). The G5 probe establishes that body tensors follow their sampled scales, not that the articulated assembly's effective pitch mode is 0.49 (`plan_update.md:135`; `finding/409`). This is Finding 1; the exact effective inertia cannot be computed from the supplied body inertias, poses, and articulation data.

No inertia-specific encoder-bound inconsistency is shown: the plan says inertia is absent from the 28D privileged vector, and the final diff keeps `state_space=28` without adding inertia (`plan_update.md:65-66,126`; `diff_envs_main.patch:679-685,743-750`). The known hardcoded-bound defect concerns payload rather than inertia (`evidence/SWEEP_native_ml_finding.md:210-215`; `finding/092`).

## 3. Control delay and paced sampling

**Verdict: REFUTED as a channel equivalence; sampler invariants CONFIRMED; pre-widening exposure CANNOT-DECIDE exactly.**

The code path is an action delay: the delayed actions replace `_actions`, and the history/energy consumers see that applied stream; privileged dim 24 reports the per-env action lag (`vert_impl_report.md:448-464`). The real observation channel is heterogeneous—joints publish at 10 Hz, attitude/gyro at no more than about 25 Hz, with 1.2-4.7 policy steps of staleness—so an episode-level action FIFO does not reproduce repeated/stale sensor samples presented to a stateful policy (`evidence/SWEEP_native_ml_finding.md:22-24,249-252`; `finding/198`; `finding/217`; `finding/264`). It can be treated as a coarse total-loop phase surrogate, but not as evidence that the observation channel is covered. If “plus ZOH” is additive as the plan states, 13+4.7=17.7 steps at the upper tail; if command-to-measured-joint already includes the ZOH component, calling it “plus” double-counts it (`plan_update.md:35-41`; `evidence/SWEEP_native_mac.md:114-115`). This is Finding 7.

The sampler implements the documented inclusive range and half-even ceiling (`diff_envs_main.patch:983-1013`). It can draw lag 13 only when `round(13s)=13`, i.e. `s >= 12.5/13 = 0.9615`, and then only one of 14 uniform integer outcomes is 13 (`diff_envs_main.patch:990-1013`). At the exact nominal `s=0`, every environment is `(0,0)`; starting at zero/very low delay is the stated pacing intent, not itself a defect (`diff_envs_main.patch:794-800`; `plan_update.md:42-49,121`). The supplied inputs do not include the per-iteration `control_delay_strength` distribution or applied-lag log, so I cannot prove whether the first 1000 iterations were literally `(0,0)` or whether any environment reached 13; the health script does not record either quantity (`p5/p5_health.sh:8-17`; `vert_impl_report.md:518-520`).

For fixed exam overrides, `lo=hi=N` makes the result exactly N for every strength, so the `--control-delay N -> (N,N)` invariance claim is correct (`diff_envs_main.patch:1009-1013`; `vert_impl_report.md:485-492`). Axis 6 explains why the scripts fail to invoke that invariant for their `dl=0` cells.

## 4. Verticals pinned dead and Fz/My disturbance

**Verdict: CANNOT-DECIDE on full-strength learnability; implementation/frame CONFIRMED; “silently observable health” REFUTED.**

The full disturbance is `13*0.1458 = 1.8954 N·m`. Against measured restoring stiffness 7.76 N·m/rad, that is a static pitch load of 0.244 rad, about 14.0 degrees (`diff_envs_main.patch:656-673`; `evidence/SWEEP_native_mac.md:100,118`; `finding/136`; `finding/155`). The only supplied authority envelope is 7.8-29.8 degrees and is explicitly artifact-derived with a fourfold thrust uncertainty, so its low end cannot reject that worst load while its high end may (`evidence/SWEEP_native_mac.md:29,125-126`; `finding/160`). No isolated rollout or full-strength curriculum trajectory is supplied, so whether the curriculum reaches and masters strength 1 is undecidable.

The strength is not mechanically stuck at zero: it is registered on `[0,1]`, starts at nominal zero, and pre-smoke already logged `Dist/strength=0.008` (`diff_envs_main.patch:768-800`; `plan_update.md:136`). The wrench writes body z force and body y moment, then calls the composer with `is_global=False`; `finding/305` says the deployed/config column map and T4 frame agree and that m0 produces pitch without roll (`diff_envs_main.patch:269-284,957-974`; `finding/305`). The actual E3 `eval.py` injector is not present in the supplied full-code diff, so its convention cannot be compared independently; the supplied spec states that it uses the same add-after-set composer path (`vert_spec.md:71-79`).

The health table does not make an Fz-only stall reliably visible. Although the environment logs `Dist/strength_mean`, the table records only random `Dist/fz_abs_mean`, then declares a freeze only if a composite of five values is exactly unchanged; fresh Fz draws or movement in any other listed dimension hides a frozen Fz strength (`vert_impl_report.md:65-70`; `p5/p5_health.sh:8-23`). This is part of Finding 6 and contradicts the claim that the table localizes a confounded six-change failure (`plan_update.md:182-185`).

## 5. Yaw heading control

**Verdict: REFUTED in reward/gate rationale; “half unreachable” REFUTED; useful plateau CANNOT-DECIDE.**

With `sigma=0.10`, the exponential at 0.3 rad is `exp(-0.3^2/(2*0.1^2)) = exp(-4.5) = 0.011`; only about `0.3/pi = 9.5%` of a uniform wrapped-error interval lies inside +/-0.3 rad (`diff_envs_main.patch:1144-1149,1160-1163`). The term is not gradient-dead, because the unchanged formula also subtracts `quad_ratio*e^2` with `quad_ratio=1`; however, at `|e|=pi` the quadratic contribution alone is `-3.5*pi^2 = -34.54`, even before the tanh penalty. That contradicts the plan's claim that the yaw floor fell only to about -10; the observed -9.0 at iteration 22 is an early mean, not the mathematical floor (`diff_envs_main.patch:1144-1149,1160-1163`; `plan_update.md:136-139`). This is Finding 2.

The geometric reachability claim is less severe than suggested. `0.55 rad/s * 5 s = 2.75 rad`; under uniform shortest-path errors on `[-pi,pi]`, the fraction needing more than five seconds at exactly 0.55 is `(pi-2.75)/pi = 12.5%`, not half. The rate term is also a soft ReLU cost rather than a hard clamp (`diff_envs_main.patch:716-724,873-881`). Acceleration, settling, faults, and limited authority may enlarge the practical fraction, but no heading-step rollout quantifies it in the supplied evidence.

`performance_lb` is an absolute feasibility gate, not a reward re-scaling control (`finding/072`; `decision/069`). The proposed `0.9*plateau` rule can therefore allow DR to widen while heading remains poor; it cannot establish task usefulness (`plan_update.md:136-139,187-190`). The early smoke proves finite execution, not a useful yaw plateau (`plan_update.md:135-138`).

Under the new sampler, their anchored control-strength draw can act on the inherited SimToReal delay range at soft/medium/hard, contaminating both the labels and the medium-tier selection score (`finding/352`; `diff_envs_main.patch:983-1013`; `p5/p5_pick.py:16-27`). ## 7. Automatic chain and checkpoint selection

**Verdict: REFUTED.**

The final-checkpoint alias is deterministic if the fallback is exercised: `p5_exam_runner.sh` may grade the latest real `model_*.pt` as `p5_10000`, while `p5_pick.py` reconstructs `model_10000.pt`; the student then fails its existence check (`p5/p5_exam_runner.sh:36-42`; `p5/p5_pick.py:14-15,27-29`; `p5/student_arm_p5.sh:20-22`). The prior 10k evidence uses final `model_9999`, showing this naming case is real in this project (`evidence/SWEEP_native_ml_finding.md:134`). This is Finding 4.

Relaunch state is unsafe. The chain removes only `P5_SMOKE_DONE/PASS`, while every later marker and `.hq/work/p5` summary remains global; exam functions skip existing summaries and completion is based only on counting them (`p5/p5_chain.sh:8-16`; `p5/p5_exam_runner.sh:20-45`; `p5/sd_exam_p5.sh:19-36`). A stale teacher-checkpoint marker releases the student, and a stale student-exam marker releases the pack (`p5/p5_pick_wait.sh:7-9`; `p5/student_arm_p5.sh:17-22`; `p5/p5_pack.sh:8-12`). Exam, student, and pack each run a fresh `ls -1dt ... | head -1`, with no run id in the marker, so a retry can splice stages across directories (`p5/p5_exam_runner.sh:32-39`; `p5/student_arm_p5.sh:20-22,47`; `p5/p5_pack.sh:10-12`). This is Finding 5 and directly matters because the plan reserves time for one relaunch (`plan_update.md:150-152`).

Failures can look like a wait rather than a failure. The exam loop ignores an eval return code after logging it and can end without an all-done/failure marker; the detached top-level chain does not supervise child exit statuses (`p5/p5_exam_runner.sh:20-29,32-47`; `p5/p5_chain.sh:10-17`). The picker pipeline has no `pipefail`, so `tee` can hide a failed picker while the student waits up to 900 minutes (`p5/p5_pick_wait.sh:5-10`; `p5/student_arm_p5.sh:17-19`).

The picker is not an acceptance gate. It averages five medium attitude values, uses rounded hard medians only to break ties, and accepts `nanmean`/`nanmedian` without a finiteness check (`p5/p5_pick.py:12-27`). That does not implement the evidence that rankings can dissolve at hard/OOD or protect the declared deployment pair34 cells from being averaged away (`evidence/SWEEP_native_ml_decision.md:61`; `evidence/SWEEP_native_ml_finding.md:146,149`; `plan_update.md:155-162`). This is Finding 8.

Finally, `require_clean` is an exact HEAD gate before student and pack. Any commit made to fold this post-launch review into the code changes HEAD and stops those already-waiting processes, despite the plan saying findings will be folded before the student (`p5/p5_common.sh:20-23`; `p5/student_arm_p5.sh:25`; `p5/p5_pack.sh:13`; `plan_update.md:173-178`). This is fail-closed, but it means the promised automatic continuation is not valid after a material review change.
